Gift Among Memories

A visual-novel-style narrative game made with RPG Maker MZ in about two weeks and published on itch.io as an HTML5 build, playable directly in the browser. The game is in Italian.

Play in your browser ↗

The story

Veronica wakes up and her boyfriend Nicola is gone. He built a time machine to relive their memories and find the perfect anniversary gift, but got trapped inside. To save him, Veronica enters the couple's memories, from their first kiss to a New Year's Eve party, and must find clues, items and access codes before the memories are erased.

What I did

  • I took the idea from a draft to a published game: narrative structure and dialogue, maps, events and interactions, UI and testing.
  • I published it on itch.io as an HTML5 build that runs without installing anything.
  • I finished a complete game alone in two weeks, choosing what to cut to reach release.
